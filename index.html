Human Media

This channel explores the human as a figure that exists within and for media environments. I.e. a human as an object, image, signal, performance, target, content, consumer of the social media. Through the collection assembled in this channel, the human is shown as a product of media logic: acting in order to be seen, behaving within predefined formats, living life on display — everything driven by reaction and effect. The collection gathers artifacts that reflect how contemporary media systems transform human presence, mental state, and inner nature into something produced, mediated, and performed.
